Hello, I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present). I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 (B.Eng., 2020-2024), where Professor Junxian He advised me during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning; my research interests include LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability.

## Academic Background
