About Me
======

I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. My academic journey has taken me through undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), where I graduated in June 2024, to pursuing my PhD in Computer Science at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) from 2024 to present.

Research Focus
------

My research centers around natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
